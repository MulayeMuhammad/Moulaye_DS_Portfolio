Mauritanian Music Notes Recognition with LSTM

Classifying traditional Mauritanian maqamat (musical modes) on the tidinit instrument using LSTM-based sequence modelling on audio signals.

2024 Audio Classification Cultural Heritage
Python TensorFlow / Keras LSTM Librosa MFCC Audio Signal Processing Sequence Classification NumPy

Project Overview

This project applies deep learning to the cultural domain: automatically recognizing and classifying traditional Mauritanian musical notes (maqamat) played on the tidinit — a traditional Mauritanian lute. Using LSTM neural networks on audio feature sequences, the model learns to distinguish between different musical modes, contributing to the digital preservation of Mauritanian intangible cultural heritage.

Cultural Context

The tidinit is one of Mauritania's most iconic traditional instruments. Mauritanian classical music is built around a system of maqamat (melodic modes/scales), each with distinct emotional and cultural connotations. Automating their recognition opens doors for music digitization, cultural education, and ethnomusicology research.

Methodology

  1. Audio Data Collection: Collected and curated audio recordings of tidinit performances across multiple maqamat.
  2. Feature Extraction: Used Librosa to extract Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs), spectral centroid, chroma features, and zero-crossing rate from audio segments.
  3. Sequence Modelling: Fed time-series feature sequences into a stacked LSTM network to capture temporal patterns characteristic of each maqam.
  4. Training: Trained with categorical cross-entropy loss, Adam optimizer, and dropout regularization to prevent overfitting on the limited dataset.
  5. Evaluation: Assessed classification accuracy, confusion matrix analysis, and per-class precision/recall across maqamat categories.

Key insight: LSTM networks effectively capture the temporal evolution of melodic patterns, outperforming static feature classifiers (SVM, Random Forest) that ignore temporal structure.

Key Results

  • Successfully classified multiple maqamat of the tidinit instrument using LSTM sequence models.
  • MFCC-based features combined with LSTM temporal modelling proved most effective for this domain.
  • Contributed a novel dataset and baseline model for Mauritanian music information retrieval research.
  • Demonstrates the applicability of deep learning to underrepresented musical traditions.

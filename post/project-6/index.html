CHAPO — INPC Management Application

A full-stack Django web application for automated management and predictive modelling of the National Consumer Price Index, built for Mauritania's National Statistics Agency.

November 2023 Government / Statistics Price Index
Django Python PostgreSQL Predictive Modelling REST API Data Automation Price Index Calculation Reporting

Project Overview

In November 2023, this project was undertaken to develop a web application for managing the National Consumer Price Index (INPC) for Mauritania's National Agency of Statistics and Demographic and Economic Analysis (ANSADE). The CHAPO application was built using Django to facilitate the systematic management of the INPC and the prices of various products across different points of sale nationwide.

Problem Statement

Effective and accurate management of consumer price data is crucial for statistical agencies to monitor inflation and guide economic policies. However, collecting, processing, and analyzing this data can be labor-intensive and prone to human error. There was a clear need for automated, reliable tools to improve the accuracy and efficiency of managing the INPC across hundreds of product categories and geographic points of sale.

Key Features

  • Automated INPC Calculation: Implements the Laspeyres price index formula automatically from raw price data inputs, eliminating manual calculation errors.
  • Predictive Modelling: Integrates time-series forecasting models to anticipate future price trends and inflation trajectories.
  • Price Data Management: Multi-user interface for data entry and validation across different points of sale and product categories.
  • Report Generation: Automated generation of detailed price fluctuation reports in standard statistical formats.
  • Historical Tracking: Full audit trail of price data over time, enabling trend analysis and year-over-year comparisons.

Objectives

  1. Develop a user-friendly application for managing the INPC workflow end-to-end.
  2. Automate price index calculations and data collection processes.
  3. Provide predictive modelling tools to anticipate price trends.
  4. Facilitate the generation of detailed and accurate reports on price fluctuations.
  5. Enhance economic decision-making through precise, real-time analysis of price data.

Impact: CHAPO reduces the time required to produce INPC reports from days to hours, improves data accuracy through automated validation checks, and provides a foundation for evidence-based economic policy at the national level.

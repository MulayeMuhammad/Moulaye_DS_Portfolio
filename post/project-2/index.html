Vulnerable Households Classification & Targeting

Identifying and targeting vulnerable households in Mauritania using survey microdata, dimensionality reduction, and neural networks.

2024 Mauritania Social Policy
Python Scikit-learn Logistic Regression PCA Artificial Neural Networks Pandas NumPy EPCVM Survey Data

Project Overview

This project uses machine learning to classify and target vulnerable households in Mauritania, leveraging the EPCVM 2019–2020 national household survey data. The goal is to build predictive models that help social policy agencies identify which households are most at risk of poverty, enabling more effective targeting of social transfers and welfare programs.

Data

The EPCVM (Enquête Permanente sur les Conditions de Vie des Ménages) is Mauritania's national household living conditions survey, covering demographics, income, expenditure, health, education, and housing conditions for a representative sample of households.

Methodology

  1. Data Preprocessing: Cleaned survey microdata, handled missing values, encoded categorical variables (region, household type, access to services).
  2. Dimensionality Reduction (PCA): Applied Principal Component Analysis to reduce correlated socioeconomic indicators into orthogonal components, retaining 85%+ variance.
  3. Logistic Regression: Baseline binary classifier to predict household vulnerability status, with decision threshold optimized for recall maximization.
  4. Artificial Neural Network: Multi-layer perceptron to capture non-linear relationships between socioeconomic features and vulnerability.
  5. Model Evaluation: Precision, recall, F1-score, and AUC-ROC — with emphasis on recall to minimize false negatives (missing truly vulnerable households).

Key Results

ANN outperforms logistic regression in capturing complex interactions between poverty indicators, while PCA reduces 40+ features to 12 components without significant information loss.

  • Identified key vulnerability drivers: household size, female-headed household status, access to clean water, and educational attainment.
  • Developed a targeting score ranking households by vulnerability probability for policy intervention prioritization.
  • Provides a replicable framework for welfare targeting in developing-country contexts using standard household survey data.

My academic journey reflects a deep commitment to mastering the intersection of data science, data engineering, economics, and advanced statistical methods.

My work encompasses predictive analytics, DSGE modeling, spatial econometrics, and the design and implementation of enterprise-scale data platforms including Data Warehouses and Data Lakehouses. I am particularly passionate about applying cutting-edge techniques in Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and distributed computing to solve real-world business challenges.

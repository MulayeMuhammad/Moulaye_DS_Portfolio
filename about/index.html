About Me

Data & AI Engineer · Statistician Economist · Machine Learning Researcher

Moulaye Ahmed Mohammed Brahim

I am a Data & AI Engineer and Statistician Economist from Mauritania, with deep expertise spanning machine learning, econometrics, and large-scale data engineering. My work bridges the quantitative rigor of economic research with the technical depth of modern AI systems.

My academic background combines advanced statistics, economic theory, and computer science — trained at INSEA (Morocco), the prestigious school of applied statistics and economics. This dual foundation allows me to tackle problems that sit at the intersection of data science and economic policy.

I have applied my skills across diverse domains: predicting poverty from satellite imagery in Nigeria, building national price index management systems for Mauritania's statistics agency, developing time-series forecasting models for financial markets, and replicating landmark econometric studies.

When I'm not building models, I'm exploring traditional Mauritanian music data (yes, really), contributing to open-source projects, and writing about applied ML and economic data science.
